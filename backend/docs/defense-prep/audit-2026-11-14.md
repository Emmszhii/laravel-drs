## D. Should fix (consistency and overclaims)

**Chapter I and front matter**
- **Weighted shortage, defined two ways.** The definition (p. ix) says "in quarters of average use", but the Stage 1 model divides by the target. Reword the definition to cover both: divided by the target in the model, or by average use in the backtest.
- **RQ3 vs Objective 3.** RQ3 ends "every … status recorded" while Objective 3 ends "every functional test case passes". Make them match one to one.
- **RQ2 vs Objective 2.** RQ2 asks about four measures; Objective 2 tests only weighted shortage. Add "and compare their fill rate, items that ran out, and budget used" to Objective 2.
- **Hash Chain definition.** It says "makes **any** later edit … detectable", but p. 17 admits a full rebuild is possible. Change it to "makes a later edit or deletion of a chained entry detectable".
- **VED definition.** It says "agreed with Logistics". Change it to "proposed by Logistics and confirmed by the Head of Office", to match pp. 12 and 15.
- **Unused definitions.** "Borrowed Request" and "Budget Allocation … across resource types" are never used in the body. Delete them, or rename to Borrowed Equipment and say "across items".
- **p. 1, "811 families (3,330 persons), 629 of whom".** It is unclear whether 629 counts families or persons. Check DSWD (2025a) and say which.
- **p. 13, "The study uses the office's records…".** Add "once released", because p. 16 says a sample dataset is used until then.
- **First uses to spell out:**
  - COA in Ch I (p. 18);
  - LGU (p. 1: "local government unit (LGU)");
