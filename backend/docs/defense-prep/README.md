## Manuscript requirements (from Classroom)

- Paper: Letter (8.5 × 11 in)
- Margins: left 1.5 in; right, top, and bottom 1.0 in
- Font: Arial, 12 pt, double-spaced, justified
- Figures and tables: APA 7th edition labels, titles, and presentation
- Hardcopy: 1 copy, ring-bound, clear plastic cover
- Before submitting, check:
  - consistency of headings and subheadings

## Time budget (student's plan, 2026-11-08)

The whole defense runs **about 1 hour**:

| Part | Time |
|---|---|
| Slide presentation (LSPU template) | 30 min |
| System demo video | 5–15 min |
| Panel Q&A | 15 min |

Sizing rules that follow from this:
- **Deck:** about 25–30 slides at roughly 1 minute each.
- **Script:** written to 30 minutes, with a per-section time mark.
- **Demo video:** cut to fit between 5 and 15 minutes; plan a 10-minute target with an optional 5-minute short cut.
- **Q&A preparation:** sized for 15 minutes, so plan crisp answers of 1–2 minutes.
