The study in one minute

CDRRMO San Pablo City plans its quarterly supply orders by hand, with no written rule. DisasterResource+ (1) plans each order from the item's own past quarters within the budget, (2) explains every order with a reason and a budget curve, and (3) keeps stock records that work offline, never count twice, and show any later edit. It also digitizes the ICS 213-RR form and the borrowed-equipment logbook.

Model: watch it, then try it

A 4-minute video of what the replenishment model does, then four things to play with. Everything uses the manuscript's illustrative worked example (Tables 9–12), not CDRRMO data.

1 · Spend the budget

Four items, targets at 1.5 × average use (Table 10). Move the budget, pick a spending method, and change each item's VED weight. At ₱200,000 with equal weights you get Table 11.

2 · Stage 1: each item's own target

Food packs' last 8 quarters (illustrative, average 320). Q4 is a typhoon quarter. Change the VED class or the typhoon quarter's size and watch the target.

3 · Stage 2: every past quarter is a scenario

Same 8 quarters, each a possible next quarter (1 in 8). Move the stock after ordering and see which scenarios run short.

4 · Budget curve

Stage 1 re-solved at 11 levels, 50% to 150% of the budget in part 1, with part 1's weights. The orange dot is the entered budget.
