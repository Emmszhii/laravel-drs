Manuscript Formatting and Submission Requirements 
Please ensure that your follows the formatting requirements below:
Paper Size: Letter (8.5 × 11 inches)
Margins: Left: 1.5 inches ; Right/Top/Bottom: 1.0 inch
Font style: Arial
Font size: 12 pt
Spacing: Double-spaced
Alignment: Justified
Figures and Tables: Follow APA 7th edition guidelines for figure and table labels, titles, and presentation.

Hardcopy Submission
Please submit:
1 copy of the manuscript
Ring-bound
With a clear plastic cover
Before submission, make sure that your manuscript is complete, properly formatted and carefully checked. Pay particular attention to:
Consistency of headings and subheadings
In-text citations and references
Tables and figures
Page numbering
Overall manuscript formatting and organization
Please review your manuscript thoroughly before submitting to ensure that it meets all the required guidelines.
